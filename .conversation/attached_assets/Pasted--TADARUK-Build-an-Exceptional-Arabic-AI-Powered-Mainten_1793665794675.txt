# 1. Understand TADARUK

TADARUK is an intelligent maintenance reporting and management platform.

It helps organizations replace scattered WhatsApp messages and Excel files with structured maintenance tickets.

Our initial demonstration focuses on a restaurant chain with multiple branches.

Each organization has its own employees, branches, technicians, and maintenance data.

TADARUK does not provide external technicians.

### How TADARUK works

1. An employee discovers a maintenance issue.
2. The employee uploads a photo and describes the problem using text or voice.
3. Voice recording is transcribed into editable text.
4. AI analyzes the description and image and creates a structured maintenance ticket.
5. The employee reviews and confirms the ticket.
6. The system automatically assigns the ticket to the appropriate technician based on branch, specialization, and availability.
7. The technician receives and handles the issue.
8. The technician documents the solution through text or voice.
9. The reporting employee confirms that the repair is successful.
10. The report closes and its resolution becomes part of the organization's knowledge base.

The manager supervises all operations and manages technicians and branches.

The manager does not need to manually approve or assign every ticket.

This is the core product concept. Build the entire experience around it.

# 10. Automatic assignment — critical business logic

After the reporter confirms the AI-generated ticket:

1. Identify the required specialization.
2. Read the branch from the reporter's profile.
3. Find active technicians assigned to that branch.
4. Match specialization.
5. Consider current workload.
6. Automatically assign the ticket.
7. Notify the technician in the application.
8. Record the assignment.

If no qualified technician is available:
- Save the ticket.
- Set assignment as pending.
- Notify the manager.

Do not fabricate an assignment.

The manager handles exceptions, not every ordinary ticket.
