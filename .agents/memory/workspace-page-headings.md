---
name: TADARUK workspace headings
description: Page-level heading treatment for internal workspace screens.
---

Internal workspace and reporter pages should show the main title directly, without the small kicker or eyebrow above it. Keep the main title and its supporting description.

**Why:** The user asked to remove the small line above titles because it repeats information and adds clutter.

**How to apply:** Use this rule in shared page-heading components across workspace and reporter tabs. Do not remove distinct section labels or landing-page copy unless asked.
